## Project: Online Seminar Marketing System

### Situation

Seminar registration triggered a chain of activities across campaigns, contacts, CRM records, webinar access, reminders, attendance, and follow-up. Managing those steps as disconnected automations made data quality and recovery difficult.

### Task

Build an in-house marketing platform that owns the seminar workflow and connects external CRM, webinar, email, and reporting services through an internal integration layer.

### Action

- Built a reusable event and registration model for seminar series and individual sessions
- Captured consent, campaign attribution, and UTM information with each registration
- Added CRM, webinar, email, and reporting adapters behind the internal workflow
- Implemented contact matching, attendance processing, confirmation, reminders, and post-event branches
- Added idempotent processing, retry handling, integration status, and manual-review paths
- Connected registration, attendance, engagement, and conversion data for campaign reporting
- Added configuration for marketing and sales teams to operate repeated seminars without rebuilding the process

### Result

Delivered an internal seminar marketing product that connects campaign acquisition to registration, attendance, follow-up, and attribution without depending on Zapier for the core business workflow.

### Technologies

Marketing API, canonical event data model, CRM integrations, webinar integrations, email automation, event processing, campaign attribution
