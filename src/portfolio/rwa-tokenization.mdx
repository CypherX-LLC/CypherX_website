## Project: RWA Tokenization for Novem

### Situation

Novem needed to connect precious-metals token contracts with reserve data, pricing, customer operations, and controlled administrative workflows.

### Task

Build and maintain the technical foundation for tokenized precious metals, including proof of reserves, settlement, pricing, wallet flows, and operational controls.

### Action

- Built precious-metals token contracts with proof-of-reserves logic
- Added daily reconciliation between token supply and vault holdings
- Connected token issuance and redemption to bank-transfer settlement
- Added pricing locks to protect purchases from price changes during settlement
- Supported wallet flows, liquidity pools, and administrative mint and burn operations
- Integrated KYC and AML processes into the platform workflow

### Result

Delivered the on-chain and off-chain foundations for a precious-metals tokenization product with reserve verification, controlled issuance, and operational reconciliation.

### Technologies

Solidity, Web3.js, precious-metals token contracts, proof of reserves, bank-transfer settlement, liquidity pools, KYC, AML, wallet integrations
