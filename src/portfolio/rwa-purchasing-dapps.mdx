## Project: Novem RWA Purchasing dApps

### Situation

Novem needed applications that let customers purchase and trade tokenized precious metals through crypto and bank-transfer workflows, with accurate pricing and clear wallet activity.

### Task

Build user-facing and administrative flows connecting token vaults, pricing, payments, wallets, liquidity, and contract operations.

### Action

- Built a token-vault purchase flow with bank-transfer settlement
- Locked precious-metals pricing during purchases to prevent pricing drift
- Released purchased tokens after confirmed bank-transfer receipt
- Created liquidity pools to support DEX swapping
- Added real-time precious-metals pricing to trading and purchase workflows
- Delivered multi-wallet support across web3 and mobile wallets, with transaction visualization and portfolio tracking
- Built an admin panel for minting and burning operations

### Result

Connected tokenized-asset purchasing and 24/7 trading flows across fiat settlement, wallets, liquidity pools, and administrative contract controls.

### Technologies

Web3.js, smart contracts, BSC, DEX liquidity pools, multi-wallet integrations, bank-transfer settlement, real-time pricing
