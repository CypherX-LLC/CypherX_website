05 / Before we talk

Fair questions.

Do we need a fully defined project?

Proposed approach: bring the challenge and what you know so far. The first conversation should establish whether an assessment, a focused build, or a different next step makes sense.

Can you work with our existing team?

CypherX’s current services include engineering leadership, delivery optimization, and distributed team building. The working model and responsibilities would be agreed for your engagement.

Is this only about AI?

No. The existing practice also covers software quality, technical due diligence, digital assets, and engineering leadership. The recommendation here is to lead with the problem, not a specific technology.

What happens after we get in touch?

This prototype links to the current CypherX contact page. Proposed flow for the redesign: a short introductory conversation, a fit check, then an agreed next step. No pricing or response-time promises are implied.
